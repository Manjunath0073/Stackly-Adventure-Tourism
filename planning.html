How we plan

Four principles.
No mystery.

A good plan makes the trade-offs visible before anyone books a flight.

01

Define the constraint

A plan starts with what cannot move: dates, budget, access, and the people who have to say yes.

03

Price the risk

Weather, permits and guide availability get a named fallback, not a hopeful note in the margin.

02

Sequence the dependencies

We put the long-lead decisions first and the nice-to-haves where they can still move.

04

Keep a review rhythm

A short weekly check keeps the plan current without turning it into another meeting.
