Selected services / How we go deeper

Built for the ground
you’re on.

Three flagship services, each shaped around a different kind of exposure: altitude, water and local relationship.

01 / Route design & guiding

A route is an argument about how a day should feel.

We build the walking day first, then fit the logistics around it. That means honest distance, clear exits and a guide briefing that leaves room for the ground truth.

  • Daily route cards with distance, elevation and exit points
  • Permit and access checks before any deposit
  • Local guide briefing and weather contingencies
What’s included

Route scoping workshop, two revision rounds, GPX files, access notes and a final guide briefing. Placeholder scope; confirmed per project.

02 / River & coast

Water sets the schedule. We plan around it.

Tides, flow and landing options decide the day. We design the route around the water’s timetable, then build in the shuttle and safety margins that make it work.

  • Tidal and flow windows for each day
  • Put-in, take-out and emergency egress notes
  • Water-specific kit and guide ratios
What’s included

Tidal planning, shuttle logistics, safety briefings, local operator liaison and a conditions call sheet. Placeholder scope; confirmed per project.

03 / Local partnerships

The best local knowledge is a relationship, not a directory.

We work with a small number of regional partners whose judgement we trust. Roles, money and responsibility are agreed before the first traveler arrives.

  • Partner selection and fair-scope agreements
  • Community and access protocol notes
  • Shared field briefing with named responsibilities
What’s included

Partner introductions, role clarity, cultural and access guidance, and a shared debrief after departure. Placeholder scope; confirmed per project.

Engagement process / From brief to ground

From first brief
to first light.

A short, direct process. No discovery theatre, no forty-page deck before the useful conversation.

  1. 01

    Frame the brief

    We define the group, the season, the comfort level and the non-negotiables.

  2. 02

    Read the ground

    Local partners and field notes test the idea against access, weather and pace.

  3. 03

    Build the route

    You get daily cards, contingencies and a clear list of what is and is not included.

  4. 04

    Travel & review

    The guide makes the call on the ground. We debrief what worked and what changes next time.

Questions / Before we start

A few useful
answers.

01How quickly can a route be built?

A focused sprint usually takes two to three weeks after the brief is confirmed. Complex multi-region work takes longer, and we will say so before starting.

02Do you guide the journeys yourselves?

We design and support them with local guides and regional partners. The guide on the ground leads the day-to-day decisions and has the final call on conditions.

03Can you work with our existing operator?

Yes, when roles and responsibilities are clear. We can join as route designers, field support or an independent review layer without duplicating the team already in place.

04What is not included in the placeholder pricing?

Travel, accommodation, permits, insurance and local partner fees are quoted separately. Prices shown are placeholders, not live quotes or a substitute for a scoped proposal.
